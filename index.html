// Dependencies

Listed dependencies

These are the major dependencies currently wired through build.dependencies.gradle and TeamCode/build.gradle.

FTC SDK 11.2.1 Inspection, Blocks, RobotCore, RobotServer, OnBotJava, Hardware, FtcCommon, and Vision.
Pedro Pathing com.pedropathing:ftc:2.1.2, telemetry:1.0.0, and ivy:1.0.0 for follower setup, visualization, and Ivy command scheduling.
FTC AutoTune pidautotuner-core:v0.3.7 plus repo-local FTC master adapters for relay-feedback PID/PIDF tuning.
UI / Telemetry Helpers com.bylazar:fullpanels:1.0.12 plus androidx.core, core-ktx, and appcompat.
TeamCode Build Tooling dev.frozenmilk:Load:0.2.4, dev.frozenmilk.sinister:Sloth:0.2.4, Kotlin Android support, and Lombok annotation processing.
PsiKit / AdvantageScope org.psilynx.psikit:core:0.2.0 and org.psilynx.psikit:ftc:0.2.0 provide the logged-mechanism and logger surface used by the host-side simulation code for AdvantageScope-oriented telemetry workflows.
JTS LocationTech Geometry org.locationtech.jts:jts-core:1.20.0 powers library/game/Field.java polygon/circle construction, robot-footprint sweeps, and autonomous-boundary contact checks.
Test Dependencies junit:4.13.2 and io.mockk:1.14.5 for unit and Android-side testing.
